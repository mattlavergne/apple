🍎 The Apple: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version: The Apple has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Your progress stays on your device unless you turn on Sync.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, scores, upgrades, apple skins, settings, daily streaks and challenges) is saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. In the app it is kept in the app's own storage, which your phone may include in its normal device backups. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes it.

Sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync, the game makes up a random 12-character code and stores a copy of your game progress on our server at mattlavergne.com under that code, so another device that enters the same code can share it. That copy contains only game progress and settings, plus random device labels the game makes up to keep each device's star count separate. It contains no name, email, phone number, advertising ID or hardware ID.

  • Anyone who knows your sync code can load and change that save, so share it only with your own devices.
  • Like any website, our server (run on Cloudflare) sees your device's IP address in order to answer a sync request. It isn't saved with your progress or used to identify you.
  • “Stop syncing on this device” stops that device from sending or receiving updates. To have a cloud copy deleted, contact us with its sync code (see below).

Sharing

The Share button only opens your device's own share sheet (or copies text) with a short result summary. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it.

Children

The Apple is made to be fun for kids and adults. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. There are no ads, chats, purchases or links to social networks inside the game.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions, or a request to delete a cloud save: reach out through mattlavergne.com.
