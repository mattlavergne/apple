🍎 The Apple: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version: The Apple has no accounts and no analytics. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Your progress stays on your device unless you turn on Sync. The free version of the app shows ads from Google that aren't personalized; buying the full game removes them.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, scores, upgrades, apple skins, settings, daily streaks and challenges) is saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. In the app it is kept in the app's own storage, which your phone may include in its normal device backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync, the game makes up a random 12-character code and stores a copy of your game progress on our server at mattlavergne.com under that code, so another device that enters the same code can share it. That copy contains only game progress (levels, stars, scores, upgrades, skins, daily results), plus random labels the game makes up to keep each device's star count separate. Your settings stay on each device. It contains no name, email, phone number, advertising ID or hardware ID.

To protect your progress from mistakes, the server also keeps a few recent earlier versions of that copy (the last 10, plus one per day for 14 days). A cloud copy that hasn't been synced for 6 months is deleted automatically, along with those versions. Your devices keep their own progress either way.

  • Anyone who knows your sync code can load and change that save, so share it only with your own devices.
  • Like any internet service, our server (run on Cloudflare) sees your device's IP address in order to answer a sync request. It isn't saved with your progress or used to identify you.
  • Delete cloud copy (on the Sync screen) deletes the cloud copy and its earlier versions right away. Other devices using that code stop syncing, and every device keeps its own progress.
  • “Stop syncing on this device” only stops that device from sending or receiving updates.

Ads (free version of the app)

The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens (never while you're playing), and an ad you can choose to watch for bonus stars.

  • Ads are not personalized. They aren't based on your interests or what you do in other apps, and they're limited to content suitable for general audiences.
  • To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects some information from your device, such as your IP address, device type and settings, and an advertising or app identifier. Google handles this under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before showing ads.
  • Buying the full game turns ads off completely.

Purchases

Buying the full game is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether the full game was bought on your store account, and remembers the answer on your device. Nothing about purchases is sent to our server.

Sharing

The Share button only opens your device's own share sheet (or copies text) with a short result summary. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it.

Children

The Apple is made to be fun for kids and adults. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, children included. The ads in the free version are not personalized and are limited to general-audience content, and a purchase always goes through the App Store's or Google Play's own confirmation. There are no chats or links to social networks inside the game.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or help with the game: email contact@mattlavergne.com.
