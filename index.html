How to play

You're the apple. The snakes are hungry. Lead them into walls, rocks, brambles, each other or their own tails. In Adventure, each level has a goal (crash every snake, survive, collect stars, or crash the golden snake). Clear it without a bite for 3★!

⬆️
MoveArrow keys / WASD. On a phone or tablet, drag anywhere on the field.
💨
Dash SpaceZip a few tiles forward. Great for escaping lunges.
🌿
Bramble EDrop a thorny bush behind you. Fresh thorns stun a chasing snake and are deadly to a lunging one. Old thorns make great walls for traps.
🦠
Hidden Rot QFrom level 3. Secretly rot for a few seconds. A snake that bites you gets sick and withers away. Run, because if it bites a fresh apple it's cured!
✨
Decoy RUnlock it in the Orchard. Leaves a shiny fake apple behind.
❗
Watch for "!"A snake about to LUNGE rears back with red eyes. Get out of the way!
⚡
NerveSlip right past a snake's nose to build NERVE. It multiplies every point you score, up to ×6. Play it safe and it cools off.
🌶️
HungerSnakes speed up the longer a level lasts: HUNGRY, then FRENZY, when they're faster than you. Each time, the hedges close in from the edges. Don't run laps: set the trap!
☠️
Your nemesisThe snake that ends your run remembers you. It ambushes a later run, stronger each time it wins. Beat it to claim its bounty.
🌟
Grades & eventsClear levels fast, unbitten and brave for an S. Many levels roll a twist: fog, quakes, a golden snake, a mirror twin…

On a phone: swipe to roll, tap to stop. Prefer holding a stick? Switch to Joystick in the menu.

★ Stars buy upgrades and skins 🌱 Seeds refill brambles ❤ Hearts heal a bite

Snakes grow longer every few seconds, so the longer you survive, the less room they have.
